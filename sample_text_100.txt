Before the library opened, an archivist discovered a folded letter behind a damaged map. Its author described a bridge that had disappeared from the city decades earlier. The location seemed familiar, but the surviving records offered conflicting dates. She compared photographs, examined a survey, and followed the river on foot. Beneath an ordinary railway crossing, she found the weathered old stone bridge foundations. By afternoon, the letter had become part of a much larger story about the neighborhood. The discovery changed no grand theory, yet it restored a small piece of history that residents could finally recognize as their own.
